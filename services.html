Before you keep reading

On a scale from 1 to 10, what level of anxiety do you actually feel when you're walking around now, under the version of yourself that you show everybody else?

Not the number you'd say out loud, but the one that's there the second you're alone and the noise stops.

Most people who ask themselves that question honestly land somewhere they don't like. And most people have been sitting at that number for so long, it stopped feeling like a problem and started feeling like their personality.

You fill the quiet with your phone, overeating, overworking, staying busy, watching TV, and saying yes to plans you don't actually want, because the quiet is where you'd actually have to feel it. Which sadly has become your baseline.

The mask you're wearing wasn't originally a lie, it began as a form of protection. At some point, it became so familiar you forgot you put it on and started calling it "just who I am."

Unapologetic is where that mask comes off, not by pretending the anxiety isn't there, but by finding out what it's actually protecting you from, and building the kind of confidence that doesn't need the mask to hold it up.

How it works

Three phases, twelve weeks.

Weeks 1–4 · Uncover

Uncover

  • Map where you edit, filter, or freeze, with real, specific examples
  • Trace the pattern back to the age and event it started at
  • Name the specific belief still running underneath it

Weeks 5–8 · Prove

Prove

  • Take the real, avoided action, the ask, the conversation, the boundary
  • Build a resourceful state before you do it
  • Treat every attempt as data, never as failure

Weeks 9–12 · Embody

Embody

  • Stop resisting your own state, so the new response becomes default
  • Practice weightlessness against other people's judgment
  • Leave with a plan that holds without me

Common questions

Before you book

Is this therapy?

No. If you've already done the therapy work and want to actually act on it instead of continuing to talk about it, that's where this picks up.

How is this different to other coaching?

Unlike traditional life or goal-oriented coaching, which focuses more on surface-level actions and quick fixes, transformational coaching explores the internal drivers behind a person's behaviour and supports the changes they need to make to achieve their desired transformation.

What happens on the discovery call?

We'll look at one area of your life where you feel stuck, name the pattern underneath it, and figure out together if Unapologetic is the right fit. Spots are limited.

How much time does this actually take each week?

You'll have one session with me each week, plus some real-world action to practice between sessions. This isn't a course you passively consume, it works because you actually do the work in your own life, not just during our calls.
