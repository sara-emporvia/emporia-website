Why Unapologetic Exists

The anxiety and self-doubt end here.

Unapologetic exists for people who feel anxious, self-doubting, or stuck in patterns of people-pleasing and procrastination, and who are exhausted from carrying it every day.

It shows up as overthinking every conversation, saying yes when you mean no, or feeling like you're performing a version of yourself instead of actually being you.

Unapologetic was created to shatter those limiting beliefs at the root, not through motivation or willpower, but through nervous-system and identity-level work that creates lasting, real change.

01

Aware

The goal is not to be told what's wrong. It's to actually see the pattern running underneath the anxiety, the people-pleasing, the procrastination, often for the first time.

02

Confident

Real confidence is built through action, not affirmations. Every time you feel the fear and do the thing anyway, you build evidence that you can handle more than you thought.

03

Consistent

Motivation is temporary relief, but identity is permanent and long-lasting, which is why Unapologetic focuses on identity-level change that makes the new response your default instead of something you have to force.

What Transformational Coaching Actually Is

Most coaching focuses on surface-level goals and action steps. This works with the root causes underneath your habits, the core beliefs and identity that decide whether a new action actually sticks or quietly fades after a few weeks.

Examines core beliefs

Identifies the hidden assumptions and patterns, often formed early, that are actually driving your daily choices.

Shifts identity

Helps you become the kind of person who naturally does the thing, instead of forcing yourself through a checklist.

Holds a safe space

Listens deeply, tolerates discomfort with you, and uses silence to let you actually reflect instead of performing an answer.

Challenges blind spots

Asks the questions that confront the limiting thought or unspoken fear you've been talking around.

Aligns values and actions

Connects what you actually do with what you actually care about, so the change holds because it's yours, not borrowed.

Meet the Founder

Hey, I'm Sara.
And I know what it feels like to be anxious and stuck.

Before I became a coach, I built my career as an environmental consultant working in contaminated land management. My job was analytical, structured, and high in responsibility. I worked long hours, managed technical reports, and operated in environments that demanded precision and competence.

From the outside, it looked like I was building a solid career.

Internally, I felt the slow erosion of alignment.

I wanted greater freedom, more control over my time, and work that aligned with my evolving values. But underneath that, I was anxious most days, quietly people-pleasing my way through a life that wasn't mine, and too afraid of what people would think if I left.

"I convinced myself I needed better timing, more savings, or a better plan. Really, I just didn't trust myself enough to feel safe."

Growth felt uncertain, and uncertainty felt dangerous, so my anxiety kept me exactly where I was, telling me that staying stuck was safer than facing the unknown.

Leaving that career was neither dramatic nor impulsive. It was uncomfortable and gradual, and it meant learning to sit with anxiety instead of letting it run the show.

That process became the foundation of Unapologetic.

Today, I am an NLP-accredited coach working with people who feel anxious, self-doubting, or stuck in people-pleasing patterns. My science background shapes how I track real change, while my personal experience guides my work on identity and nervous-system healing.

I do not believe people are broken. I believe their nervous system is protecting them. Once we understand this protection, we can create supportive structures rather than resist it.

Why this is different

What I see that
most coaches miss.

I work with what you don't realise you're saying

After my NLP training I became unable to ignore the difference between what people say they want and how they actually talk about their lives. The words people use reveal everything about how they see themselves. Most people have no idea their own language is keeping them anxious and stuck, and that's almost always where the real work needs to happen.

I don't treat the symptom

Most coaching addresses the behaviour. The procrastination, the avoidance, the overthinking. I address what's underneath it. When we identify and shift the internal safety mechanism driving the pattern, the behaviour changes because the reason for it has changed. That's why the results hold.

I came from the same place

I've lived with anxiety and people-pleasing myself, and I spent years feeling stuck in patterns I couldn't name. That experience isn't just a story I tell. It's the reason I recognize these patterns quickly in the people I work with, because I lived inside them for a long time too.

Every tool I give you is made for you

There are no generic worksheets or frameworks here. Your pattern work is built for your specific block. Your action plan maps your particular way of avoiding. Everything is built around your situation, not a template.
