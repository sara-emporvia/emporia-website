Meet the founder

Who is Sara?

Sara didn't start her career as a transformational coach, it began with hiding. For years, she moved through life as a master of disguise: outwardly polished, inwardly unravelling. Anxious and driven by people-pleasing, she built a corporate career she was told to want and clung to a 6-year relationship long after the spark faded, terrified that choosing herself would mean letting others down. Every choice was a performance, every smile a carefully crafted mask.

Then, in 2024, everything changed. Sara walked away from the career that had defined her, and, in the same breath, ended the relationship that had anchored her for years. In one bold, excruciating season, she lost the identities she'd spent a lifetime protecting. The world she'd carefully built crumbled, leaving her stripped bare, utterly alone with the uncomfortable truth of herself, and unable to hide behind busyness or expectation. For the first time, she was forced to meet herself, unfiltered and unguarded.

That's where the real transformation began, not with surface-level hacks or empty affirmations, but with the gritty, honest work of facing her shadows. She peeled back layers of old stories and coping mechanisms she never knew she'd been using, confronting the ways she'd been protecting herself from pain. Sara had spent a lifetime playing a part and calling it "who I am." Now, she started to discover who she was underneath.

Today, Sara's mission is to guide others through the same courageous unmasking. She helps people lay down the heavy armour of perfection and people-pleasing and finally come home to the selves they abandoned long ago. It's not about becoming someone new, it's about remembering who you were before the world told you to hide.
